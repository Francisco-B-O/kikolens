# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want KikoLens to be a place where people of every background and level of experience can ask questions, report problems and contribute
without being treated badly. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
* Assume good faith, and explain disagreements with arguments rather than attacks.
* Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
* Respect other people's time: say what you tried, keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Insults, personal attacks, trolling or deliberately provoking others.
* Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
* Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
* Anything that would be reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider harmful. To report a problem, write to the maintainer at the address on
their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
